The Ritual

Patience is the first ingredient.

01
Heat to 85°C
Never boiling. Water too hot destroys the delicate catechins and the subtle floral notes that define a true Assam.
02
Steep for 3 minutes
Use 2 grams per 150ml. Allow the leaves to unfurl completely — watch the amber bloom into the water.
03
Presence
Put the phone down. This cup took 200 years to arrive at your hands. Give it a moment.
